What the results mean

A nearby SSID is only a radio-visible network. A mapped hotspot is a directory entry. A Dream Wi-Fi source is a device registered with Dream Wi-Fi. Only a participating source that is online, authorized, and passing connectivity tests can potentially provide remote internet.
