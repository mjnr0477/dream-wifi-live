Find participating Wi-Fi services.

Dream Wi-Fi detects your phone’s location and lists participating Dream Wi-Fi source devices reported within 300 km. It does not need Supabase or an owner login. A source must be online and authorized to share its connection before it can relay traffic.

Important limits

Not a universal Wi-Fi scanner

A website cannot scan every nearby SSID or detect every router across 300 km. This list contains only participating Dream Wi-Fi source devices with location coordinates.

Connection availability

A hotspot is not connectable just because it appears on the list. Its source device must remain online and its owner must have explicitly authorized sharing. This website does not yet route all phone traffic as a full VPN.
