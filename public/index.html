Prototype testing notice

Dream Wi-Fi is still being tested. Full-phone VPN routing, reliable background operation, traffic-leak prevention, and sharing internet to a third phone through the receiver’s hotspot have not passed physical-device acceptance tests. A map result does not itself connect you to Wi-Fi. Please do not rely on this prototype for sensitive traffic.

Good to know

Finding a place is not the same as connecting.

Public listings may be closed, paid, password-protected, or out of date. Dream Wi-Fi cannot reveal passwords or grant access to private networks.

Only share networks you are allowed to share.

A sharing device must be online and explicitly authorized. Background operation and full-device routing still need real-phone testing.

Your location is used for search.

When you search, your coordinates are sent to a public OpenStreetMap-compatible map service to find mapped Wi-Fi venues. You choose whether to allow location access.

Dream Wi-Fi is a standalone product, separate from Dream Education.
