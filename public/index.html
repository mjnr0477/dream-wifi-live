Important limits

Not every router or SSID

No website can remotely scan every private router or every Wi-Fi network across 300 km. Public results are mapped venues tagged as offering Wi-Fi; coverage depends on map data. They may be closed, password-protected, paid, or no longer available.

Finding a hotspot is not connecting

A map listing does not provide its password or authorize access. To relay traffic through Dream Wi-Fi, a participating source device must be online and explicitly sharing its network. The web app is not a full-device VPN.

Location privacy

Your coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's public Overpass map-data service to look for mapped Wi-Fi venues. Use location search only if you're comfortable with that request.
