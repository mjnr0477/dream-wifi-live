Terms and acceptable use

Prototype service: Dream Wi-Fi has not completed all required physical-device, privacy, and production acceptance tests. Availability, performance, device compatibility, full-device VPN routing, and hotspot forwarding are not guaranteed.

Use only with authorization

  • Only enroll a source device and share a Wi-Fi or mobile connection when you own it or have explicit permission from the network owner and provider.
  • Only use pairing codes and receiver sessions that the source owner has intentionally provided to you. Keep codes, tokens, and credentials private.
  • Do not use the service to access private routers, local devices, accounts, or networks without authorization; bypass payment, access controls, captive portals, or provider restrictions; or conduct unlawful, abusive, fraudulent, or harmful activity.
  • Do not use the service to transmit malware, attack systems, evade lawful restrictions, or interfere with other users or network providers.
  • Stop sharing if you are unsure whether the network permits it. Sharing may consume bandwidth, battery, or mobile data and may incur charges.

Technical limitations

Public Wi-Fi map results are third-party map listings, not a guarantee that a venue is open, free, secure, or accessible. A participating source's online status or source-side reachability probe does not prove end-to-end browsing from a receiver. The relay supports TCP and UDP destinations subject to safeguards; ICMP and other non-TCP/UDP IP protocols are not forwarded. The native Android VPN, background reliability, no-leak behavior, and third-device hotspot forwarding have not completed physical acceptance testing. Do not assume the receiver joins the source's Wi-Fi or that a receiver VPN automatically shares internet with a third phone.

No guarantee or warranty

The service is provided as a development prototype, subject to interruption, bugs, security limitations, data loss, and incompatible networks or devices. Do not rely on it for emergency communications, confidential activity, financial transactions, or other high-risk uses. You remain responsible for your device, network permissions, and compliance with applicable law and provider terms.

Suspension and security

The operator may disable source enrollment, revoke pairings, or suspend service to protect users or investigate abuse. Report suspected misuse through support and abuse reporting. Do not include credentials or private data in a public report.
